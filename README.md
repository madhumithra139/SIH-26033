## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution
Project Title: AI-Based Digital Marketplace for Farmers
Our proposed solution is an AI-powered digital marketplace that connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers through a single online platform.

The main objective of this platform is to help farmers sell their agricultural products directly to buyers without depending on multiple intermediaries.
Key Features of the Proposed Solution
1. Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Connection

Farmers can register on the platform and upload details of their agricultural products, including product name, quantity, quality, price, and availability.

Consumers, retailers, hotels, supermarkets, and bulk buyers can browse the available products and place orders directly with farmers.

2. AI-Based Demand Forecasting

The system uses Artificial Intelligence to analyze previous sales, seasonal trends, market demand, and consumer purchasing patterns.

Based on this information, the AI predicts which agricultural products will be in demand in the future.

This helps farmers make better decisions about what crops to grow and how much to produce.

3. Smart Logistics and Transportation

The platform provides transportation support by connecting farmers with available delivery vehicles and logistics providers.

It helps organize the collection, storage, and delivery of agricultural products from farms to buyers.

4. AI-Based Route Optimization

The system uses AI to identify efficient delivery routes based on distance, traffic conditions, delivery locations, and vehicle availability.

This reduces transportation time and delivery costs.

5. Digital Payment and Price Transparency

The platform provides secure digital payment options and displays product prices clearly.

Farmers and buyers can negotiate prices directly, ensuring transparency and reducing unnecessary commission charges.

6. Multilingual and User-Friendly Interface

The platform supports regional languages such as Tamil and English, making it easy for farmers to use.

A simple interface with voice assistance can also help farmers who have difficulty using digital applications.

2. How It Addresses the Problem
The current agricultural supply chain faces several problems, including middlemen, low prices for farmers, high transportation costs, food wastage, and a lack of market information.

Our proposed solution addresses these problems in the following ways:
Existing Problem	Proposed Solution
Farmers receive low prices	Direct connection with consumers and bulk buyers
High commission charges	Reduced dependence on intermediaries
Lack of market demand information	AI-based demand forecasting
High transportation costs	Smart logistics and route optimization
Delayed delivery of products	Efficient transportation planning
Agricultural product wastage	Better demand prediction and timely delivery
Lack of price transparency	Real-time product and price information

Overall Impact
The proposed platform creates a more efficient agricultural supply chain by connecting farmers directly with buyers and using AI to improve production planning and distribution.

It helps farmers earn better income, allows consumers to purchase fresh products at reasonable prices, and reduces unnecessary costs in the agricultural supply chain.

3. Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution
The uniqueness of our solution lies in combining a digital marketplace, Artificial Intelligence, and logistics management into a single platform designed specifically for the agricultural sector.
Innovative Features
1. AI-Powered Market Prediction

Unlike traditional marketplaces, our platform predicts future demand using historical sales data, seasonal patterns, and market trends.

This helps farmers plan their agricultural production according to market requirements.

2. Smart Farmer-to-Buyer Matching

The system automatically matches farmers with suitable buyers based on product type, quantity, location, quality, and price preferences.

This reduces the time required to find potential customers.

3. Intelligent Logistics Management

The platform combines orders from nearby farmers and buyers to improve transportation efficiency.

AI-based route optimization helps reduce fuel consumption, delivery time, and transportation expenses.

4. Fair Price Recommendation System

The system analyzes market prices, demand, and product availability to suggest a reasonable selling price for farmers.

This helps farmers make informed pricing decisions.

5. Regional Language Accessibility

The platform supports local languages and a simple user interface, making digital agricultural services accessible to farmers from different educational backgrounds.

6. Sustainable Agricultural Supply Chain

By improving demand prediction and delivery planning, the platform can reduce food wastage, unnecessary transportation, and resource consumption.
Conclusion
Our AI-Based Digital Marketplace for Farmers provides a smart, transparent, and efficient solution to agricultural marketing problems.

By integrating direct market access, AI demand forecasting, fair price recommendations, and intelligent logistics, the platform aims to empower farmers, benefit consumers, and build a more sustainable agricultural supply chain.
